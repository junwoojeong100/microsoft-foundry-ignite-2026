# Frequently asked questions

This is a fictional FAQ written for lab practice.

## What if I cannot sign in?

Tell the operator. Do not sign in with another account or a personal account.

## What if I get a 429 (rate limit) error?

Do not keep resending the same question. Wait 1 minute and try again once; if it continues, ask the operator to check your TPM allocation.

## Where can I charge my laptop?

Use the power strip under each table. Bring your own charger.
