Login and MFA guide screenshots
As of: 2026-11-18

Every PNG in this folder was captured for this repository. No pictures from external documents are used.

How they were captured
- A new test account that has only the three participant roles (RG Contributor, RG Foundry User, and Cognitive Services Usages Reader on the subscription)
  signed in to the Azure Portal for the first time. The screens were captured in a Chromium browser at 1600x900.
- Only the sign-in card area was cropped. No resizing or other editing was done.

Masking
- The sign-in email was replaced with user@contoso.com before capture. The real UPN is not in any picture.
- The email, password and verification code input fields were blurred during capture.
- The QR picture and the manual registration key (Secret key) were covered with gray boxes. You cannot register an app from these pictures.
- The pictures contain no real password, QR code, Secret key or verification code.

Files
- signin-1-upn.png to signin-12-return-code.png (number 7 does not exist): the sign-in and MFA registration sequence (the first sign-in lab of the guide)
- foundry-auto-setup.png: the "Setting up your project" screen shown when the Foundry portal (ai.azure.com) is opened for the first time
